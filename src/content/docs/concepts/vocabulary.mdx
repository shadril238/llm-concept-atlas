## Picture it

Imagine a dictionary where each entry is a text piece paired with an integer. The tokenizer looks up a sequence of pieces, and the model receives their integer IDs.

## Why it matters

The vocabulary determines which pieces are readily available. Different tokenizers can represent the same text with different numbers of tokens.

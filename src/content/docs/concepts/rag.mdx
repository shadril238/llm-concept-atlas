## Picture it

For a question about a company's policy, the system first retrieves relevant policy passages. The generator then answers using those passages as context, ideally showing sources the reader can inspect.

## Why it matters

RAG connects a model to information outside its training data. Its quality depends on retrieval, context selection, generation, and evaluation together.

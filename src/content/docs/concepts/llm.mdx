## Picture it

Give a model the start of a sentence. A generative LLM predicts a plausible continuation, one token at a time. Other language models make useful representations of the sentence instead.

## Why it matters

The word “large” is a moving description. Architecture, training, and the task you give a model are more useful than size alone when choosing one.

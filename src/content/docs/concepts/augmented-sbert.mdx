## Definition and intuition

Augmented SBERT uses a cross-encoder teacher to label additional sentence pairs, then trains a bi-encoder student on gold and teacher-generated silver pairs. Silver labels are predictions with provenance and possible errors, rather than newly discovered ground truth.

Follow the input, objective, update, and evaluation as separate steps. The worked example and experiment below connect the explanation to an inspectable implementation.

## How it works

### Train the teacher on gold

L16 actually trains a tiny BERT pair classifier on the original gold training pairs and selects its checkpoint on gold validation cross entropy. The teacher jointly encodes both sentences and outputs a same-intent probability. Its initial/updated hashes and first encoder gradient are checked, and its saved model is reloaded. This includes teacher training, unlike an inference-only shortcut.

### Keep candidate provenance separate

Candidate silver pairs are sampled uniformly from a separate 24-sentence training-only pool, without using its latent intent labels. Every pair retains sentence IDs, selection policy, teacher score and origin. Validation/test sentences never enter silver generation. Latent author labels are inspected only afterward as a diagnostic of teacher mistakes.

### Compare two real students

Gold-only and gold-plus-silver students start from the same pretrained encoder and use the same 60-step regression budget and validation rule. The augmented mixture changes data exposure, so it is not a pure isolated label-quality intervention. Evaluate on untouched gold test pairs and retrieval queries; a strong teacher or more labeled pairs does not guarantee a better student.

## Worked example

In the first author run the trained teacher disagreed with the latent relation on 14 of 120 silver candidates at a diagnostic threshold of 0.5. The student still receives continuous probabilities. Its gold-plus-silver test MSE was worse than gold-only, providing a concrete example of augmentation failing rather than a silently selected success.

import json
from pathlib import Path
data = json.loads(Path('src/data/training-results.json').read_text())['augmentation']
print('silver candidates:', len(data['silver']))
print('teacher diagnostic disagreements:', sum(r['disagreement'] for r in data['diagnostics']))

    print(row['condition'], 'test MSE:', round(row['similarity']['mse'], 4))

## When to use it

Use it when a reliable pair teacher can provide economical extra supervision for a fast searchable student. Inspect silver quality and sampling coverage before assuming more pairs help.

## Limits and common mistakes

Teacher errors, probability miscalibration, class imbalance, near-duplicate candidates and evaluation contamination can all harm the student. Gold annotation itself has a task policy and uncertainty; silver should not be represented as independently human-verified.

Author execution checks tensor/mask contracts, actual parameter updates, split provenance and artifact reload. It does not constitute independent lesson acceptance. Keep training quality, task quality, runtime and generalization claims separate.

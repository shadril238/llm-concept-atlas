## Picture it

In “The dog chased the ball because it was excited,” attention can help the representation of “it” use information from earlier words. A heatmap can show the relative weights for one head in one layer.

## Why it matters

Attention is one way transformer tokens exchange context. A bright heatmap cell is a weight in a computation, not a complete explanation of a model's reasoning.

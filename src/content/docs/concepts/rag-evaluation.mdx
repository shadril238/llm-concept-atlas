## Definition and intuition

RAG evaluation separates retrieval relevance, answer correctness, context faithfulness, citation support, fluency, and usefulness. A system can succeed on one dimension and fail on another, so no single score should stand in for all of them.

Read this lesson as a sequence: identify the input, follow the transformation, inspect the result, and explain a failure. The worked example and recorded lab below make those boundaries visible.

## How it works

### Separate the stages

L12 measures ranked retrieval with document-level judgments. L13 additionally records required-support-document recall, raw generated drafts, source IDs, and application withholding. A substring probe is explicitly only a diagnostic: the right number may refer to the wrong entity, and a paraphrase can be correct without matching the reference string. Author review supplies proposition judgments rather than pretending string checks establish semantics.

### Citation precision and citation recall

Precision asks whether cited passages contribute support to their associated claims. Recall asks how many generated external-world claims are fully supported by their citation sets. Multi-hop claims can need two sources jointly; one useful partial citation does not make the whole statement fully supported. No citations gives undefined precision under this policy; no answer propositions gives undefined recall, not a perfect score.

### Human and optional model judgment

The original book cites human verifiability evaluation and discusses automated judges. The atlas includes an explicit author rubric and an offline optional judge importer with output hashes and provenance. Uncertain entailment remains uncertain. A model judge may share errors with the answer generator, follow source instructions, or vary with wording. Imported judge labels do not become independent truth, and no live judge execution is claimed.

## Worked example

A draft answering the assistance-dog question describes a staircase and elevator. Those statements can be faithful to the supplied access passage while irrelevant to the requested animal rule. Another draft correctly says 17:30 but cites nothing. The citation exercise adds a Wren→Birch→42 claim to show why complete joint support differs from per-link usefulness.

## When to use it

Use stage-specific evaluation to decide what to repair: ingestion, ranking, candidate coverage, context selection, generation, citation linkage, or display policy. Keep actual failure cases and human rationales available alongside aggregate metrics.

## Limits and common mistakes

One implementing author is not an independent review panel. Labels can be ambiguous, especially for vague or incomplete answers. The mechanism probes are small and already inspected; no untouched generative benchmark, calibrated judge, or universal quality score is claimed.

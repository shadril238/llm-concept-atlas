## Picture it

A user searches for “how to lower power use.” A relevant document titled “Energy efficiency tips” may still match well even though it uses different words.

## Why it matters

Dense retrieval can find related meaning, while keyword matching remains useful for exact names and terms. Many practical search systems combine methods.

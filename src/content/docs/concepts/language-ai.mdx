## Picture it

A spam filter reads an email and assigns a label. A translation system turns one sentence into another language. A language model continues a paragraph. These are different tasks within the same field.

## Why it matters

“Language AI” names the broad problem space. A large language model is one kind of tool within it, not the whole field.

Start with a goal, then follow its concepts, experiment and project. The suggested sequences cover the main dependencies; each lesson also lists its complete declared prerequisites. You can jump to a later topic and return to its prerequisites when needed.

Reading and practice checkmarks are **your own study notes**, not a test score or certification. Existing reading checkmarks are migrated automatically; the original browser record is retained. Progress is not uploaded or shared across devices. [Coverage and verification](/llm-concept-atlas/coverage/) describe a different question: what the study guide implements and what has actually been checked.
